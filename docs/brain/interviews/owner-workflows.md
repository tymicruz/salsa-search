# Owner Research Workflows

Date: 2026-11-16

Purpose: Record how the owner researches a salsa scene, step by step, as general workflows rather than facts about any one city.

## Workflow 1: listing → Google place → Facebook → flyer (verbatim)

I don't know where I'm going with this, but I was just clicking the randomized button on our UI just to get a random city. I came across Ho Chi Minh or something like that. I looked up the city in SalsaVida, and I see an event. It's at this place.

Then I look up that place on Google because eventually we want to mark this place. On the Google place page, or whatever you call it, there is a Facebook link. I click the Facebook link, and I go to the photos. Then I see the flyer for the salsa event too.

This isn't just strictly a salsa place, which is common. Some places just do other stuff. It might be a coffee shop, and then it can host. They might host one salsa event, and during the week that's a good event for salsa.

That's just one example, one kind of workflow of how I would like to find an event, but this needs to be scaled up for many of the places in a city and then also many cities.

**Notes (interviewer)**

- Steps: event listing site → venue name → Google place page → Facebook link on that page → Facebook photos → event flyer.
- The flyer is an image, so reading it needs AI that can read images.
- Many venues aren't salsa places: a café or bar may host one good weekly salsa night.
- It has to scale to many venues per city and many cities.

## Workflow 2: Google Maps searches are noisy (verbatim)

Right now, I'm just clicking around, doing things manually. One thing I'm doing is doing a Google Maps search: I just type the city name and then "salsa school." A lot of the time, a lot of options pop up that are dance schools or Latin dancing schools, but there's not really salsa there, or literally no salsa.

For example, this one has tango classes, ballet clásico classes, and some wedding choreography. There's a lot of that, and there's no salsa. It's just that kind of thing. Those are some things I want to filter out.

Also, I just typed in "salsa dancing," and there are a lot of places that might do salsa dancing a little bit, but not really. It's just not a really good method. Just searching "salsa dancing" is not in Google Maps. It's not a good method for seeing where the events are.

so thats why like clicking thru as a human and kind of looking at many references as a human gives me a better picture. now i'm trying my best to commmunicate my ingenuity as a human on how to do this non trivial task of finding quality and not even quality but just places to dance salsa and join the local scene

**Notes (interviewer)**

- A Google Maps search for "salsa school" returns many dance schools with no salsa at all (tango, ballet, wedding choreography). These need filtering out.
- A Google Maps search for "salsa dancing" returns places that barely do salsa. It's a poor way to find events.
- The human method is to click through and compare many references to build the picture. No single search answers it.
- The goal comes before quality: first find places where you can actually dance salsa and join the local scene.
