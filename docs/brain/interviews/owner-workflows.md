# Owner Research Workflows

Date: 2026-11-16

Purpose: Record how the owner researches a salsa scene, step by step, as general workflows rather than facts about any one city.

## Workflow 1: listing → Google place → Facebook → flyer (verbatim)

I don't know where I'm going with this, but I was just clicking the randomized button on our UI just to get a random city. I came across Ho Chi Minh or something like that. I looked up the city in SalsaVida, and I see an event. It's at this place.

Then I look up that place on Google because eventually we want to mark this place. On the Google place page, or whatever you call it, there is a Facebook link. I click the Facebook link, and I go to the photos. Then I see the flyer for the salsa event too.

This isn't just strictly a salsa place, which is common. Some places just do other stuff. It might be a coffee shop, and then it can host. They might host one salsa event, and during the week that's a good event for salsa.

That's just one example, one kind of workflow of how I would like to find an event, but this needs to be scaled up for many of the places in a city and then also many cities.

**Notes (interviewer)**

- Steps: event listing site → venue name → Google place page → Facebook link on that page → Facebook photos → event flyer.
- The flyer is an image, so reading it needs AI that can read images.
- Many venues aren't salsa places: a café or bar may host one good weekly salsa night.
- It has to scale to many venues per city and many cities.
