## B. Places

### 1. PM La Casa del Bailador (Rinconada)
- Kind: social dancing (class + social)
- Partner dancing: yes
- Day/time: SalsaVida says every Tue and Fri, 8pm class, then a 9pm salsa/bachata social until 2am, $100 MXN. Wanderlog shows Fri 7pm–2am only, so the days conflict.
- Who it suits: not stated
- Crowd: not stated. Wanderlog's own summary mentions "lots of regulars".
- Vibe: "My favourite place in Puerto Escondido to dance salsa & bachata! … I absolutly love the socials!" (Google review, Jan 2025, via Wanderlog). Also: "Great place to dance salsa & bachata in Puerto! Friendly staff, good classes, tasty drinks, great music" (Oct 2024). Rated 4.9 from 43 Google reviews.
- Sources (checked 2026-11-16): [SalsaVida](https://www.salsavida.com/event/mexico/oaxaca/puerto-escondido/salsa-nights-at-pm-la-casa-del-bailador/) (step 1), [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/11131406/pm-la-casa-de-bailador) (step 2). Instagram @pmlacasadelbailador was blocked.
- Status: confirmed by 2+ sources

### 2. Terraza Zikaru (Marina Nacional 18, Hidalgo, near Bahía Principal)

- Who it suits: not stated

- Sources: [SalsaVida](https://www.salsavida.com/?p=24828) (step 1), [VivaPuerto salsa](https://vivapuerto.com/vp9/salsa.php) (step 1), [VivaPuerto dance](https://vivapuerto.com/vp11/dance11.php) (step 2). Instagram @congobarmx was blocked.

## D. Blind spots
- **Google Maps reviews:** JS-only, so the step 2 playbook basically failed. School ratings, review counts and review-named places came only via Wanderlog for one venue.
- **Reddit:** fully blocked, and search engines surfaced no threads. Locals-vs-tourists and vibe data are thin.
- **Instagram:** most profiles were unreadable. I couldn't confirm whether Mándala, Congo, Grupo Fiebre and PM La Casa still run these nights, or who teaches them. No instructor accounts could be followed.
- **SalsaVida list is truncated:** the header claims 34 events in 30 days, but only 7 recurring listings were visible. There may be more behind "Load More".
- **PM La Casa days conflict:** Tue and Fri (SalsaVida) versus Fri only (Wanderlog hours).
- **Current dedicated school:** none found. Esesarte and Los Bravos are from an undated, old article. A local school (likely the one behind PM La Casa or the Zikaru/Néctar classes) probably exists but is unnamed publicly.
- **Old venues:** Kabbalah, Rockaway, El Mexicano and La Pared may be closed; no current sources.
- **Salsa Fest:** current status and dates unknown.
- **Puerto Knights:** this live band played Thursdays at PM La Casa per a 2024 review. Where it plays now is unknown.
- **Crowd mix:** only Congo, Néctar and La Pared have any locals/tourists statement. Local-only spots (e.g. salones in the town center, away from Zicatela) are likely under-represented, since every source is tourist-facing.
